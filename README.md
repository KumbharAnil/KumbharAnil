## 🧬 About Me

I am **Anil Kumbhar**, an M.Sc. Medical Biotechnology student interested in **Computational Drug Discovery, Bioinformatics, and Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)**.

My interests combine molecular modeling, cheminformatics, biological data analysis, and computational genomics to investigate biological systems and support drug discovery research.

### 🔬 Research Interests

🧪 **Computational Drug Discovery**

* QSAR and q-RASAR modeling
* Molecular docking and virtual screening
* Molecular dynamics simulations
* ADMET profiling and molecular property analysis
* Structure–activity relationship studies

🧬 **NGS Bioinformatics & Transcriptomics**

* RNA-seq data analysis
* NGS data processing and quality control
* Read trimming and genome alignment
* Gene expression quantification
* Differential gene expression analysis
* Cancer transcriptomics and biological interpretation

💻 **Programming & Computational Analysis**

* Python and R for scientific computing
* Linux and command-line bioinformatics
* Data processing, visualization, and statistical analysis
* Reproducible computational workflows

### 🎯 My Goal

My goal is to develop reproducible computational workflows that connect **chemical data, molecular modeling, and high-throughput biological data** to generate meaningful research insights.

I am particularly interested in integrating computational drug discovery with transcriptomics and other omics approaches to better understand disease biology and investigate potential therapeutic targets.
